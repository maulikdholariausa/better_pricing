# Have a Guru — privacy policy and terms for completion

Prepared November 8, 2026 from the current intake implementation and the proposed matching service. These drafts require the factual fields below before replacement of the public policies. Do not represent them as approved legal advice or completed operational controls.

## Required operator decisions

- Legal entity or individual operating Have a Guru; business mailing address; operating state/country.
- Verified support and privacy-request email, monitored by the operator.
- Retention periods for inactive submissions, source documents, consent records and backups; deletion procedure and responsible person.
- Manual versus automated matching workflow; who can access source documents; how original prices and other contractors' identity are withheld before disclosure.
- Whether contractors pay for leads, contact access or another service; recipients and purposes of disclosures; applicable sale/share opt-out and Global Privacy Control handling.
- Any AI/OCR, communication, analytics or other processors actually used; human access and international transfers.
- Intended minimum age and launch geography; any contractor-specific commercial agreement.

## Privacy Policy — substantive draft

### Operator and scope

### Information collected
You provide your phone number, contractor estimates and other project details. Uploaded documents may contain addresses, pricing, signatures or information about other people; remove unrelated information and provide only documents you are authorized to share. Intake records include file names, types, sizes, submission time, reference identifier and the consent text/version. Infrastructure may process IP addresses and request/device information for routing, security and abuse prevention. Optional analytics, when configured and accepted, records limited page and funnel events; the application does not intentionally send quote contents, phone numbers, file names or submission references to analytics.

### Purposes
Use information to receive and administer submissions, understand the requested work, identify potentially relevant contractors, facilitate authorized project contact, provide support, secure the service, keep consent records and comply with applicable obligations. Do not use this policy as authorization for unrelated marketing or new data uses.

### Sharing and contractor contact
With the authorization presented at submission, relevant project details and the customer's phone number may be disclosed to matched contractors so they can discuss the project and arrange appointments or estimates. Explain actual review/redaction practices before promising that original pricing or unrelated information will never be disclosed. Infrastructure and service providers process information to operate the service, subject to the actual contracts in place. Information may also be disclosed when legally required, to protect rights or in a business transaction subject to applicable law. Contractors handle received information under their own responsibilities and policies. A withdrawal can stop future disclosures but cannot undo information already received by another party.

### Paid lead access and privacy choices
[CONFIRM THE BUSINESS MODEL.] If contractors pay for personal-information access, assess whether this constitutes a sale under applicable law. Do not state “we never sell your data” without that assessment. Where applicable, disclose categories, purposes and recipients; provide working opt-out methods and honor required preference signals. This clause must match implemented processes, not merely appear as a promise on the site.

### Cookies and analytics
The site can store an analytics preference in browser local storage. An optional external analytics tag loads only after opt-in when a measurement ID has been configured. Visitors may decline or turn it off using site controls. Confirm actual provider settings, cookies and retention before enabling analytics. Describe other tracking only if actually used. Do not claim automated honoring of all browser privacy signals unless implemented and verified.

### Retention and security
Keep information only for the defined operational, legal and security purposes. Publish [RETENTION CRITERIA/PERIODS] and the backup-expiry treatment after the operator chooses and implements them. Use reasonable safeguards; no system is guaranteed secure. The current application keeps database and source files outside the public web directory; access and backup practices must be reviewed independently.

### Requests and rights
Contact [PRIVACY EMAIL] to request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of future sharing authorization or other applicable rights. Verify identity proportionately, avoid requesting unnecessary sensitive documents, and respond within applicable deadlines. Describe exceptions and appeals if applicable. This policy must not promise a response workflow that nobody monitors.

### Children, location and changes

Contractors independently decide whether to respond, inspect or quote. Unless separately agreed, Have a Guru is not the construction contractor and does not perform, supervise, insure or warrant construction work. Do not imply verification or background screening beyond checks actually completed.

### Eligibility and submissions
Users must be [APPROVED AGE] and authorized to submit the materials and arrange the project. Provide accurate information, check restrictions in existing contracts, and omit unrelated sensitive data. Grant only the rights needed to receive, store, review and process materials and share permitted project information according to the displayed consent and privacy policy. ### Contact authorization
Contractor-sharing authorization is the consent displayed during submission and recorded with the request. It is not blanket authorization for unrelated or automated marketing. Contact frequency, channels and any later marketing permissions must comply with applicable requirements. Explain the actual withdrawal channel once configured.

### No guaranteed outcome
Submitting a quote does not guarantee a match, response time, lower price, project award or contractor performance. Information and educational materials do not replace a site inspection or professional advice. Users compare scope, qualifications, insurance, schedule, payment and warranty terms and enter construction agreements directly with the chosen provider.

Do not upload unlawful or malicious materials, impersonate others, submit unauthorized documents, attempt to access private records or abuse the service. Access may be restricted to protect users and systems, subject to applicable law and any obligations regarding existing transactions.

### Responsibility, disputes and updates
Use balanced limitations subject to nonwaivable rights; do not disclaim liability where the law does not permit it. Operator-specific governing law, venue, dispute process and any liability provisions require jurisdiction-specific review. Provide a monitored contact for questions and complaints.
